Delete your Greynote account

This page is for field employees using the Greynote app. You can also delete your account inside the app: Profile → Delete account.

What is deleted

  • Your name, phone number, email, address, personal and bank details, and uploaded documents.
  • Your sign-in. You will not be able to use the app with this account again.

What is kept

  • Payroll, visit, order and expense records, without your personal details (shown as “Deleted user”). The company must keep them for tax and audit.

This cannot be undone. Managers with active team members must have the team moved first.

Cannot sign in or need help? Contact your HR team and ask them to delete the account for you.
